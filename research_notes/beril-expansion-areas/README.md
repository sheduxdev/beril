## How to read

- `[H]` means a primary source or several sources. `[M]` means one secondary source. `[L]` means an aggregator or conflicting source.
- Price figures are rough and often third party. Verify before quoting.
- The repo was young at the time. Some line counts and data counts have moved since. Treat the weakness file as a snapshot.
- The security market file was written in Turkish and translated to English on 26 November 2026. Links and confidence labels were kept.
