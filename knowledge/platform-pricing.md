# Internal pricing is a signal, not a profit center

## Claim

Showback informs; chargeback transfers budget. A price that hides shared cost distorts demand.

## Test

Map the unit and the allocation rule. Compare a team's usage with the bill.

## Proof

A price the team can act on.

## Failure modes

A per-request price misses idle capacity. A flat share hides the heavy user.
