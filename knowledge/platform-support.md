# Support needs a tier, a channel and an SLA

## Claim

A platform without a support path is a gate. Tiers separate a broken deploy from a question.

## Test

Read the support docs. Open one incident and one question. Measure the response.

## Proof

A response inside the stated SLA.

## Failure modes

An office-hours-only channel fails an incident. A chat without a tier floods.
