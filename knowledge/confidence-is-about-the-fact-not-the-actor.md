# Confidence is about the fact, not the actor

## Claim

A high confidence actor attribution does not make every fact high confidence. The fact grade stands on its own evidence.

## Test

Separate the actor claim and the fact claim. Grade each. Add the evidence behind the fact.

## Proof

Two grades with separate reasons.

## Failure modes

A shared tool can link actors wrongly. A single source can repeat the same claim.
