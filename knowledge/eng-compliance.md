# Compliance is evidence of a control

## Claim

The control exists, runs and leaves an artifact. A policy document is not the control.

## Test

Map the control to the evidence. Sample the period. Check the owner.

## Proof

An evidence trail for the sampled period.

## Failure modes

A tool that logs nothing proves nothing. A control without an owner decays.
