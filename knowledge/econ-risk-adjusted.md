# Risk-adjusted value multiplies by probability

## Claim

A return is weighted by the chance and the cost of failure. A high upside with a low probability can lose.

## Test

Write the scenarios, the probabilities and the downside.

## Proof

An expected value with the downside stated.

## Failure modes

A point estimate without probability is a wish. A small probability of a huge loss can dominate.
