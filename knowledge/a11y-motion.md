# Reduced motion is a vestibular setting

## Claim

Large parallax, zoom and spin can cause nausea. A reduce query removes or shortens them.

## Test

Enable reduced motion. Trigger the animation. Check the movement.

## Proof

A static or short transition.

## Failure modes

A small fade is usually fine. A scroll-jacked page is not.
