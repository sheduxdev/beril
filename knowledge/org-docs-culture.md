# Writing makes decisions visible and reviewable

## Claim

A short memo with the problem, options and decision reduces meetings and preserves context.

## Test

Read three recent decision docs. Check whether the tradeoff is stated.

## Proof

A reader can reconstruct the choice.

## Failure modes

A status update without a decision is noise. A doc nobody reviews is a diary.
