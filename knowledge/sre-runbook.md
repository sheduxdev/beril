# A runbook is a tested procedure with a decision point

## Claim

The steps name the signal, the action and the expected result. Toil above a budget is automated.

## Test

Follow a runbook on a staging alert. Time it and note the gaps.

## Proof

A procedure completed by someone on call for the first time.

## Failure modes

A runbook that assumes context fails at 3am. Untested automation is a liability.
