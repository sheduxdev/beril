# A blameless postmortem turns incidents into learning

## Claim

The timeline, contributing factors and actions matter. Blame stops the second-order detail.

## Test

Read three postmortems. Check whether people admit mistakes and actions close.

## Proof

An action with an owner and a date that closed.

## Failure modes

A postmortem that names a person closes early. An action without a date is a wish.
