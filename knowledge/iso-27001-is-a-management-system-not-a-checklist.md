# ISO 27001 is a management system, not a checklist

## Claim

The standard asks for a risk process, a statement of applicability and a management review. A control list without the process fails.

## Test

Read the risk register and the SoA. Check the review minutes. Find a control not linked to a risk.

## Proof

A risk to control trace for one sample control.

## Failure modes

A mature ISMS has the trace. A small scope can reduce the controls.
