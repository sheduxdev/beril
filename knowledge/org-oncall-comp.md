# On-call is work and must be paid or traded

## Claim

Compensation, time off and rotation fairness decide whether on-call is sustainable.

## Test

Count pages per person. Check the compensation and the recovery time.

## Proof

A rotation with fair load and a recovery rule.

## Failure modes

An unpaid 24/7 rotation burns out. A single hero hides the load.
