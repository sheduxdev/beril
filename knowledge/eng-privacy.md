# Privacy is data minimization with a retention clock

## Claim

Collect only what the feature needs, keep it only as long as needed, and make deletion real.

## Test

List the fields. Ask why each exists. Check retention and deletion.

## Proof

A data map with a deletion path.

## Failure modes

A privacy policy does not delete data for you.
