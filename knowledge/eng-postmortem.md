# A postmortem is blameless and actionable

## Claim

The timeline, the contributing factors and the actions matter. Blame stops learning.

## Test

Write the timeline. Ask why five times. Assign owners and dates.

## Proof

Actions with owners that close.

## Failure modes

An action without a date is a wish.
