# Unit economics make cost a design signal

## Claim

Cost per request, per tenant and per feature shows which design choice pays.

## Test

Allocate cost to a unit. Compare with the price. Find the dominant term.

## Proof

A unit cost and a target.

## Failure modes

A monthly bill hides the unit. Shared cost needs an allocation rule.
