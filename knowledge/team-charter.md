# A charter names the mission, the boundary and the interface

## Claim

The team knows what it owns, what it does not and how others request work.

## Test

Read the charter. Ask a neighbor what the team owns.

## Proof

A neighbor answers correctly from the charter.

## Failure modes

A charter that lists everything owns nothing. A missing escalation path creates shadow work.
