# Sunk cost is spent; only the forward cost matters

## Claim

The money is gone. Continue only if the remaining cost beats the remaining value.

## Test

Write the forward cost and the forward value. Ignore the spent amount.

## Proof

A continue or kill decision on forward numbers.

## Failure modes

A big past spend makes a bad project feel mandatory. A small past spend can hide a big future.
