# A handover names the owner and the next check

## Claim

A report that ends at the finding leaves the fix owner guessing. The handover needs an owner, a deadline and the next check.

## Test

Read the last page. Name the owner, the patch window and the retest date. Remove any item without an owner.

## Proof

A handover with three named owners and dates.

## Failure modes

A small team can share one owner. A vendor fix can block the date.
