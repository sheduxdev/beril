# A scope letter names the stop condition

## Claim

A scope without a stop condition and an emergency contact turns a finding into a negotiation after the fact.

## Test

Read the letter. Check target, window, allowed and forbidden actions, contact and stop rule. Fill every blank before the first packet.

## Proof

A signed letter with all six fields.

## Failure modes

A master service agreement can cover the blanks. A verbal scope is not a letter.
