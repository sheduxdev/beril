# A team budget is maintenance plus a bet

## Claim

Maintenance, on-call and debt consume capacity. Innovation without a protected slice starves.

## Test

Measure maintenance share. Protect a bet slice. Review monthly.

## Proof

A capacity plan that names both.

## Failure modes

A 100% feature plan hides the maintenance tax.
