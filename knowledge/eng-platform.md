# A platform is a paved road, not a gate

## Claim

Golden paths make the right thing easy and observable. A gate without a path pushes teams around it.

## Test

Follow the path as a new team. Measure the time to first deploy.

## Proof

A service created and deployed without a ticket.

## Failure modes

A platform that only enforces policy is a tax.
