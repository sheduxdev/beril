# Ask until the blocker is clear

## Claim

A hint at the right level unblocks. A lecture hides the gap.

## Test

Ask what they expect. Ask what happened. Give one hint. Ask for the next step.

## Proof

The learner produces the evidence.

## Failure modes

Solving it for them removes the practice.
