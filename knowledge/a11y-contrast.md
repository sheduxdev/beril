# Contrast is a ratio, and color is not the only cue

## Claim

Text needs 4.5:1 for normal size. A chart needs a pattern or a label, not only hue.

## Test

Measure the ratio. Convert to grayscale. Check the non-color cue.

## Proof

A passing ratio and a second cue.

## Failure modes

A large text threshold is lower, not optional. A border can carry the cue.
