# Dark web data needs a collection date

## Claim

A market listing, a leak and a forum post change or vanish. The date, the capture and the seller are part of the claim.

## Test

Capture the page and the seller. Record the date and the price. Compare with a second collector.

## Proof

A dated capture with the seller and the source.

## Failure modes

A screenshot can be edited. A seller can relist under a new name.
