# Disclosure is a timeline with a paper trail

## Claim

A vendor may stay silent, patch quietly, or dispute. The timeline and the evidence decide the outcome.

## Test

Record the first report, the acknowledgement, the fix and the public date. Keep every message.

## Proof

A disclosure timeline a third party can verify.

## Failure modes

A public dump before a fix can burn the reporter. A vendor silence is not acceptance.
