# Pricing is a signal about unit cost and margin

## Claim

A price below cost attracts the wrong demand. A price above value loses the buyer.

## Test

Compute the unit cost and the value. Compare with the market.

## Proof

A price with a stated margin and a value case.

## Failure modes

A discount for a long contract is a lock. A free tier without a cost cap is a subsidy.
