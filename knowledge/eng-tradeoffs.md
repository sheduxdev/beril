# A tradeoff without a cost is marketing

## Claim

State what improves, what worsens and who pays. Then choose.

## Test

Write two options and their costs. Pick one.

## Proof

A decision record with the rejected option.

## Failure modes

A choice that claims all benefits has no tradeoff.
