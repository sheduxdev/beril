# An air gap has a removable bridge

## Claim

The gap is only as strong as the USB, the laptop and the backup that cross it. The bridge is the target.

## Test

Map every path across the gap. Check the media, the scan host and the backup. Test the scan host.

## Proof

A path from the IT side to the OT side.

## Failure modes

A data diode is one way. A strict media policy and a scan kiosk narrow it.
