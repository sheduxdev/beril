# Containment trades evidence for spread

## Claim

Isolating a host stops spread and kills the attacker's live view. The order depends on whether the adversary is watching.

## Test

Name the spread rate, the evidence value and the business cost. Pick network isolation, host isolation or watch and wait.

## Proof

A containment decision with the three inputs written down.

## Failure modes

A fast worm needs isolation now. A quiet actor can be watched for scope.
