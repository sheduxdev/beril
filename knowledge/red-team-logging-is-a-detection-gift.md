# Red team logging is a detection gift

## Claim

The operation log, the timestamps and the tool versions let the blue team replay the path and write rules.

## Test

Record the action, the host, the time and the artifact. Hand the timeline over at the debrief.

## Proof

A timeline that matches the blue team events.

## Failure modes

A clock skew breaks the match. A hidden action is hard to hand over.
