# The untested section is part of the finding

## Claim

What was not tested bounds the claim. A report without it invites the reader to assume full coverage.

## Test

Write the untested list before the findings. Check that every finding's precondition appears in the tested list.

## Proof

A finding whose claim stays inside the tested set.

## Failure modes

A generic list copied across reports. An untested item that actually hides the main risk.
