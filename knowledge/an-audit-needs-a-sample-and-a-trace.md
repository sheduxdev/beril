# An audit needs a sample and a trace

## Claim

An auditor tests a sample against a criterion. The evidence needs the sample, the result and the link to the control.

## Test

Pick a control and a sample. Record the criterion, the test and the result. Link the artifact to the control id.

## Proof

A sample record with a control link.

## Failure modes

A small sample can be challenged. A trace without a result is a folder.
