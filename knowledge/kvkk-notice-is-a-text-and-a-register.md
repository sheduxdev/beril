# KVKK notice is a text and a register

## Claim

A data controller needs a notice, a register entry and a retention rule. A scan or a dossier can trigger all three.

## Test

Check the notice, the register and the retention. Map the collected fields to each.

## Proof

A notice and a register entry that cover the fields.

## Failure modes

A foreign controller can have a representative duty. A small volume can still be personal data.
