# Technical debt is principal plus interest

## Claim

The shortcut saves time now and slows every later change. Interest is the recurring tax.

## Test

Estimate the weekly tax. Compare with the repayment cost.

## Proof

A repayment plan where interest exceeds the fix cost.

## Failure modes

A debt with no interest is a deferred choice, not debt. A rewrite can be more debt.
