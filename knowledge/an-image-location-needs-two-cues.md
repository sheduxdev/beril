# An image location needs two cues

## Claim

One landmark can exist in several cities. A sign language, a plate style, a shadow and a map match together narrow it.

## Test

List every cue. Remove the weakest. Check the shadow against the claimed time. Match the street view.

## Proof

Two independent cues and a map match.

## Failure modes

A chain brand appears everywhere. A stock image has no EXIF.
