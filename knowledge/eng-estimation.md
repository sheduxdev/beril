# An estimate is a range and its assumptions

## Claim

Known work, unknown work and coordination have different uncertainty. A point estimate hides it.

## Test

List the three buckets. Give a range. Name the risk that doubles it.

## Proof

A range a planner can use.

## Failure modes

An estimate without assumptions is a guess.
