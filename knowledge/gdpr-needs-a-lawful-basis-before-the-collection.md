# GDPR needs a lawful basis before the collection

## Claim

A scan that collects personal data needs a basis, a purpose and a retention. Legitimate interest needs a written balancing test.

## Test

List the personal fields. Name the basis and the purpose. Write the balancing test and the deletion date.

## Proof

A record with the basis, the purpose and the retention.

## Failure modes

A security purpose can fall under legitimate interest. A special category needs more.
