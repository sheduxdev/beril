# A roadmap is a set of bets with kill criteria

## Claim

Each bet names the outcome, the leading indicator and the condition to stop.

## Test

Write the bet and the kill signal. Review the signal on a date.

## Proof

A bet stopped or doubled on evidence.

## Failure modes

A list of features is not a roadmap. A bet without a kill signal runs forever.
