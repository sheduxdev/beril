# Total cost includes the invisible line items

## Claim

Migration, training, support, audit and exit sit outside the invoice. A cheap option can be the expensive one.

## Test

List every line item for three years. Price the migration and the exit.

## Proof

A total that includes the hidden work.

## Failure modes

A license comparison alone hides the operations cost.
