# Payout is a negotiation with evidence

## Claim

The VRT and the program table set the range. The impact narrative and the chain decide where in the range it lands.

## Test

Map the finding to the VRT. Cite the impact. Compare similar disclosed reports.

## Proof

A severity a triager accepts with the evidence.

## Failure modes

A high CVSS on a low-value asset oversells. A chain without proof does not.
