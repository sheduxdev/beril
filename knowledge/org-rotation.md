# A rotation needs a handoff and shadowing

## Claim

Short rotations with a written handoff and a shadow reduce context loss and load spikes.

## Test

Read the handoff template. Check the shadow overlap and the page load.

## Proof

A handoff with open items and a shadowed first shift.

## Failure modes

A one-week rotation with no handoff repeats discovery. A permanent hero hides the process.
