# CRA reporting is a clock with a 24 hour start

## Claim

An actively exploited vulnerability needs an early warning within 24 hours and a full report later. The clock starts at awareness, not at the fix.

## Test

Write the awareness timestamp and the two deadlines. Name the reporter and the platform. Run a tabletop.

## Proof

A tabletop with timestamps and a named owner.

## Failure modes

A non commercial OSS project can be out of scope. A supplier can hold the awareness.
