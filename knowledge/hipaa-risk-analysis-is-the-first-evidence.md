# HIPAA risk analysis is the first evidence

## Claim

The Security Rule expects a documented risk analysis and a risk management plan. A policy binder without them is thin.

## Test

Read the risk analysis and the plan. Map one safeguard to one risk. Check the review date.

## Proof

A risk to safeguard trace with a date.

## Failure modes

A small practice still needs the analysis. A business associate can hold part.
