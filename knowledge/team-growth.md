# Growth needs a hiring bar and a ramp plan

## Claim

Every hire raises or lowers the bar. The ramp plan names the first deliverable and the reviewer.

## Test

Read the last three hires' ramp plans. Check the first deliverable date.

## Proof

A new hire shipping inside the ramp window.

## Failure modes

A hire without a ramp adds load to the team. A lowered bar is permanent.
