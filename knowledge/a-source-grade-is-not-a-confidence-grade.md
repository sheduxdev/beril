# A source grade is not a confidence grade

## Claim

A primary vendor report and a tweet both mention a hash. The source grade and the confidence in the fact are different axes.

## Test

Grade the source A, B or C. Grade the fact high, medium or low. Split the two in the record.

## Proof

Two fields, not one, with a reason.

## Failure modes

A good source can be wrong. A poor source can quote a primary document.
