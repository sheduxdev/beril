# A handoff needs a written owner, state and next step

## Claim

The old owner documents the system, the open risks and the contacts, then shadows the new owner.

## Test

Read the handoff. Have the new owner run one change alone.

## Proof

A change by the new owner with the old owner available.

## Failure modes

A handoff that is a meeting is lost context. A shadow with no checklist misses the risks.
