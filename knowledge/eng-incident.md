# Stabilize before you diagnose

## Claim

Rollback, flag off, drain or fail over. Diagnosis can wait until users are safe.

## Test

Name the stabilization. Assign commander and scribe. Time-stamp decisions.

## Proof

A stable system with a log.

## Failure modes

A root-cause hunt during an outage extends the outage.
