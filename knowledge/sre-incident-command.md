# Incident command separates the lead from the fixers

## Claim

The incident commander coordinates, the ops lead fixes, the scribe records, the comms lead updates.

## Test

Declare an incident. Assign roles. Check the update cadence.

## Proof

A timeline with role-based updates.

## Failure modes

Everyone debugging means no one communicates. A silent commander blocks decisions.
