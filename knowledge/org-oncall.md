# On-call load is a reliability input

## Claim

A noisy rotation burns the team and hides the real reliability work. Page volume is a metric.

## Test

Count pages per week and per person. Classify actionable vs noise. Fix the top noise source.

## Proof

A page budget and a lower noise rate.

## Failure modes

A dashboard alert is not a page. A rotation without handoff loses context.
