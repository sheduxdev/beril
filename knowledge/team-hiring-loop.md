# A hiring loop needs calibration before it decides

## Claim

Interviewers score independently, the loop calibrates on evidence, and the bar is compared across candidates.

## Test

Read the scorecards before the debrief. Check whether the decision cites evidence.

## Proof

A decision traceable to evidence, not seniority.

## Failure modes

A debrief that anchors on the first speaker. An uncalibrated bar drifts with the market.
