# An interview samples the job, not trivia

## Claim

A work sample, a review exercise and a system design probe predict the job better than puzzles.

## Test

Define the signal per interview. Score independently before the debrief.

## Proof

A scorecard tied to a job behavior.

## Failure modes

A puzzle measures puzzle practice. A debrief anchors on the loudest voice.
