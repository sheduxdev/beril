# Headcount is a recurring cost with a ramp

## Claim

Salary is only part. Recruiting, onboarding, tools and management add, and the ramp delays output.

## Test

Cost a role for a year with the ramp and the overhead.

## Proof

A fully loaded cost and a time to productivity.

## Failure modes

An FTE as a one-time cost undersells it. A senior hire with no onboarding is still slow.
