# A manager's span and coaching decide the team's rate

## Claim

Too many reports means no coaching. A career path without a next step loses the senior people.

## Test

Count the reports. Read one career plan. Check the feedback cadence.

## Proof

A report with a next step and a feedback loop.

## Failure modes

A player-coach with ten reports does neither well. A promotion without scope is a title.
