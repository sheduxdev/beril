# Fitts's law sizes a target by distance and width

## Claim

Time to point grows with distance and shrinks with width. A small far target is slow.

## Test

Measure a far small target and a near large one. Compare the time and the error.

## Proof

A target size that matches the frequency and distance.

## Failure modes

A huge button in the center is not always right. A destructive button next to a safe one needs separation.
