# A corporate registry is the ownership record

## Claim

The registry names the legal entity, the officers and the address. A brand page is marketing.

## Test

Search the exact legal title in the registry. Read the filing. Compare the officer with the site and the certificate.

## Proof

A filing that names the entity and the officer.

## Failure modes

A holding company can hide the brand. A registry can lag a change.
