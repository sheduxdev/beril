# Coordinated disclosure is a clock and a channel

## Claim

The vendor needs a private channel, a repro and a disclosure date. A public drop without notice burns the relationship.

## Test

Write the channel, the repro and the proposed date. Send the first note. Track the acknowledgement.

## Proof

An acknowledged report with a date.

## Failure modes

A vendor can go silent. A 90 day norm can be short for a large fix.
