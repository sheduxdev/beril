# Delegation needs a decision right and a boundary

## Claim

A delegated decision names the owner, the consulted, the informed and the limit.

## Test

Write a DACI for one decision. Check the limit and the escalation path.

## Proof

A decision made at the right level without a round trip.

## Failure modes

Delegation without a limit is abdication. A consulted list that is too large stalls.
