# Technical conflict needs a decision rule, not a vote

## Claim

The owner decides, the disagreeing party records the objection, and a deadline ends the debate.

## Test

Watch one contested decision. Check the owner and the record.

## Proof

A decision with a recorded dissent and a date.

## Failure modes

A consensus requirement stalls. An escalation without a rule becomes political.
