# A chaos experiment has a steady-state hypothesis

## Claim

The hypothesis names the expected behavior. The experiment injects one fault and measures.

## Test

Write the hypothesis. Inject a latency or a pod kill. Compare.

## Proof

A confirmed or refuted hypothesis with evidence.

## Failure modes

A random kill without a hypothesis is a demo. Production needs a blast-radius limit.
