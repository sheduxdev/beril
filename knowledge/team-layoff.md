# A layoff needs a scope cut and an honest story

## Claim

Cut the roadmap to what the remaining team can own, name the reason, and protect the people who stay.

## Test

Read the post-layoff plan. Check whether the roadmap shrank with the team.

## Proof

A roadmap the remaining team can deliver.

## Failure modes

A layoff with the same roadmap burns the survivors. A silent cut breeds rumor.
