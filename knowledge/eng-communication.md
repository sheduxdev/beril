# A decision nobody understands is a future incident

## Claim

Write the problem, the options, the tradeoff and the decision. A short memo beats a meeting.

## Test

Write a one-page decision record. Ask a reader to restate it.

## Proof

A reader can explain the tradeoff.

## Failure modes

A status update without a decision is noise.
